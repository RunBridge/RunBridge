Use your treadmill's distance directly on your Garmin watch.

RunBridge plugs into USB power, connects to your FTMS-enabled treadmill, and exposes speed & distance to your Garmin watch as a standard footpod. No more guessing, no more mismatched distances.

Quick Start

Follow these steps the first time you use RunBridge:

Setup
  1. Plug in RunBridge.
    Connect the USB-C port to a 5V USB power source (wall adapter, treadmill USB, or powered hub).
  2. Turn on your treadmill.
    Make sure your treadmill console is powered and broadcasting FTMS over Bluetooth.
  3. Pair on your Garmin watch.
    On your watch, go to Settings → Sensors & Accessories → Add New → Foot Pod (or Running Speed/Cadence) and select the device named RunBridge (or your configured name).
  4. Start an indoor run.
    Start an Indoor Run activity. Once your treadmill session begins, pace and distance should match what the treadmill reports.

If your watch already has another footpod paired, you may need to disable or remove it so Garmin uses RunBridge as the primary source.

Prefer a full walkthrough? See the complete quick start at Docs/QuickStart .

LED States

The XIAO nRF52840 board uses a single RGB LED to show what RunBridge is doing:

State XIAO nRF52840 LED
System Ready Solid green
Watch subscribed + treadmill connected
Treadmill Only Double blink red
2 short red blinks every ~2 seconds (waiting for watch)
Watch Connected Fast blink magenta
On/off every ~150 ms (watch connected, not yet subscribed)
Scanning Slow blink blue
1 second on / 1 second off (watch ready, searching for treadmill)
Idle / Sleep Off
No active connections

For a deeper technical breakdown, see Docs/LED-States .

Troubleshooting

If something isn’t working, start here:

Common issues
  • My watch doesn’t see RunBridge.
    Make sure RunBridge is powered and the LED is blinking (any pattern). If the LED is completely off, it’s idle: unplug and plug it back in, then retry Settings → Sensors & Accessories → Add New → Foot Pod.
  • Treadmill distance doesn’t change on the watch.
    Confirm your treadmill supports FTMS over Bluetooth and that its Bluetooth is turned on. Some treadmills only broadcast once a workout has started.
  • Distance on watch doesn’t match treadmill.
    Make sure the watch is using RunBridge as the primary footpod. If needed, temporarily disable other footpods or Stryd sensors.
  • Everything is stuck / LED pattern looks wrong.
    Unplug RunBridge, wait 5 seconds, and plug it back in. Then restart the activity on your watch. During a normal run you should settle into the System Ready state (solid green).

Full troubleshooting guide: Docs/Troubleshooting

Still stuck? Take a photo of your treadmill console and LED, and email it to support@runbridge.dev.

Compatibility

Designed for
  • Garmin watches that support Footpod / Running Speed & Cadence sensors.
  • Indoor treadmills that broadcast FTMS over Bluetooth.
  • Standard 5V USB power sources (wall adapter, treadmill USB, powered hub).
Not a good fit for
  • Non-FTMS treadmills with only proprietary Bluetooth apps.
  • Watches or devices that cannot pair to a Bluetooth footpod.
  • Environments without safe, stable USB power.

Full compatibility details (including an optional FTMS check using the nRF Connect app): Docs/Compatibility

FAQ

Does this store or upload any of my data?
No. RunBridge simply relays speed and distance from your treadmill to your watch as a standard BLE sensor. Your activities live in Garmin Connect, just like a normal run.
Can I update the firmware?
Firmware is shipped in a stable, production-ready state and is not intended for end-user updates. If a critical issue is found, we’ll publish guidance in the Docs.
What if it doesn’t work with my setup?
Reach out to support@runbridge.dev. If we can’t get it working with your treadmill and watch, we’ll work with you on a return or refund according to the Support & Policies .
