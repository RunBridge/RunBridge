Product Identification

Product Name: RunBridge
Product Model: RunBridge (all hardware revisions)
Product Type: Bluetooth Low Energy fitness bridge device
Description: RunBridge is a small USB-powered device that receives treadmill FTMS data and transmits running speed and distance data to compatible sports watches using Bluetooth Low Energy.

Product Architecture

RunBridge is built using the following major components:

Wireless Microcontroller

Nordic Semiconductor nRF52840 system-on-chip — Bluetooth 5 low energy radio transceiver with integrated ARM Cortex-M4 processor.

Development Platform

Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 module providing:

  • Bluetooth 5.0 connectivity
  • onboard antenna
  • USB-C power interface
  • low-power operation suitable for wearable and IoT devices.

The device operates using Bluetooth Low Energy in the 2.4 GHz ISM band.

Power and Electrical Characteristics

Power input: USB 5V
Operating voltage: 3.3V internal logic
Wireless band: 2.4 GHz Bluetooth Low Energy

The device does not contain high-voltage circuits and is powered by standard USB power sources.

Intended Use

RunBridge is intended for indoor fitness use and connects to compatible treadmills and sports watches.

Typical use:

  • treadmill running
  • indoor training environments
  • consumer fitness monitoring
