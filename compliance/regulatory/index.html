Regulatory Marks & Safety

Compliance marks

The following regulatory marks may appear on the RunBridge product or packaging:

  • CE Mark – Conformity with EU legislation (Radio Equipment Directive and applicable directives).
  • UKCA Mark – Conformity with UK Radio Equipment Regulations 2017.
  • WEEE crossed-out wheeled bin – Product must not be disposed of with household waste; recycle via appropriate e-waste channels.
  • FCC Identification – For United States compliance. This device contains FCC ID: Z4T-XIAONRF52840 and IC: 27958-XIAONRF52840. This device complies with Part 15 of the FCC Rules. Operation is subject to the following two conditions: (1) This device may not cause harmful interference. (2) This device must accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.

Safety instructions

RunBridge is intended for indoor fitness use only. It provides speed and distance information to compatible sports watches and is not a medical device. It is not intended for safety-critical use.

  • Use only with compatible treadmills and watches in a safe indoor environment.
  • Always follow treadmill manufacturer safety guidelines and use caution when training.
  • Do not use in wet or hazardous environments. The device is USB-powered; avoid exposing it to moisture.

Recycling and disposal

RunBridge is an electrical and electronic product. Do not dispose of it with household waste. In the European Union and United Kingdom, and in other regions with similar requirements, it should be recycled through appropriate electronic waste (WEEE) collection and recycling channels. Contact your local authority or retailer for e-waste collection points.
