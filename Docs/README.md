## What is RunBridge?

RunBridge is a small hardware bridge that:

- Connects to your FTMS-enabled treadmill over Bluetooth.
- Shows up on your Garmin watch as a normal footpod / running speed & cadence sensor.
- Relays speed and distance so your watch can use the treadmill’s numbers instead of guessing.

It’s a small-batch, hand-assembled device built for indoor runners who care about accurate data.
