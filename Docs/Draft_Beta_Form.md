## Section 2: Compatibility Check (Crucial)

*RunBridge requires your treadmill to support the standard "Fitness Machine Service" (FTMS) over Bluetooth. Please follow these steps to check:*

1.  Download **nRF Connect for Mobile** (free on iOS/Android).
2.  Enable Bluetooth on your phone and treadmill.
3.  Open nRF Connect and tap **SCAN**.
4.  Find your treadmill in the list and tap **CONNECT**.
